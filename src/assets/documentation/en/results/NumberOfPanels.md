## Recommended number of panels

This recommendation is based on the following factors.

### 1 Your daily electricity use
Since PLN can no longer buy back electricity you generate but don't use, the goal is to size your system
to cover your own daily load - not to overproduce. We size the array so that, combined with your battery,
it covers as much of your day and night usage as the sun and your battery allow, with a little headroom
(the PV oversize factor) for cloudier days.

### 2 Your battery
Solar panels only generate while the sun is up. Anything you don't use directly during the day is stored
in your battery for use at night; anything that doesn't fit in the battery is curtailed (wasted), since it
can no longer be sold back to the grid. A larger battery lets you use more of what you generate.

### 3 Location of your house
How often the sun shines in your part of the country affects the amount of electricity your panels will
produce. The more sun, the fewer panels you need.
