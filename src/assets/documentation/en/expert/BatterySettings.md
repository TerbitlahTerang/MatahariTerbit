## Battery settings

Since PLN can no longer buy back electricity you don't use right away, a battery is what lets you actually
benefit from solar power generated while you're not using it - storing daytime surplus for use at night,
instead of it going to waste.

| Setting                 | What it means                                                                 |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Chemistry                | LiFePO₄ (lithium) lasts longer and is more efficient, but costs more upfront. Lead-acid is cheaper but wears out faster. |
| Usable depth of discharge | How much of the battery's rated capacity you can actually draw on without shortening its life. |
| Round-trip efficiency    | How much of the energy you put into the battery you get back out - some is always lost as heat. |
| Price per usable kWh     | What one kWh of *usable* storage costs installed, not the raw nameplate capacity. |
| Lifetime                 | How many years before the battery needs replacing. Lead-acid batteries typically need this far sooner than lithium. |

Choosing a chemistry fills in sensible defaults for the other fields, which you can still fine-tune
afterwards.
